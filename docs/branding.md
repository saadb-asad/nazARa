# Business Name: NazARa

## Selected Name: **NazARa**

*   **Meaning**: "Nazara" (نظارہ) means "Scenery", "View", or "Sight" in Urdu.
*   **Concept**: It perfectly describes the product's function: giving customers a clear "view" or "scenery" of the food.
*   **Stylization**: **NazARa** (Capitalizing 'AR' to highlight Augmented Reality technology).
*   **Tagline Ideas**:
    *   *Scan. View. Taste.*
    *   *Apna Khana, Naya Nazara.* (Your food, a new view).
